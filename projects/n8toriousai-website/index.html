About

N8torious AI is the studio of Nathan Brown — one builder, working end to end: kernel to interface, language to storefront. The through-line is sovereignty. Software should run on the owner's hardware, keep the owner's data on the owner's device, and take its orders from the owner.

That is not a slogan here; it is an engineering rule, written into the kernel constitution the systems run on: humans are sources of authority; systems never are.
