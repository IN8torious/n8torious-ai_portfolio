   the inner gateway system. The Ball is that core, close-up — the

   HiveMind, PainTr, Gaven, TitanBoa. The kernel's five services wear
   Vera/Gaven/Witness faces; the Ball's dock shows the same crew's

## Contradictions — real ones, with resolutions

1. **Place vs instrument.** Raijin is a place you travel (galaxy →
   descend → core → Deep Alchemy gateway out). The Ball is an instrument,
   not a place. *Resolution:* the prototype stays an instrument. It
   borrows Raijin's navigation words only where travel is real (lens
   travel at the merge), and grows no fake galaxy shell.
2. **Generative amnesia vs the ledger.** Altaminac's musicode regenerates
   everything from mulberry32 seeds; nothing persists. The receipt ledger
   is append-only; everything persists. *Resolution* (extends the first
   cross-examination's promotion law): generated things are signals until
   a receipt promotes them. The swarm may regenerate freely and must
   never write a ledger entry.
3. **Two physics, two layers.** The thesis weighs words (MMC: vowel
   energy lifts, consonant mass sinks; proven against the RaijinLang
   reference, VOID = 172 REU). P2.8 forces weigh proposals, by countable
   kernel formula. Both are real; they contradict only if mixed.
   *Resolution:* thesis physics stays in the Forge (P5). It is not a
   force input, and the gaze card shows no MMC numbers until the Forge
   exists to compute them honestly.
4. **Darctix has no authority to give.** Darctix Core is an ambient
   desktop layer — mood, embers, no mechanics. *Resolution:* it
   contributes ambience only (embers off the furnace). Its helix counts
   as evidence (agreement 7), not as a system.
5. **The component CSV describes a central orchestrator. We don't have
   one.** Its six rows (Planner, Executor, Critic/Evaluator, Memory,
   Orchestration Layer, Feedback Loop) assume an orchestration layer
   routing everything — against the Devuan crew law and the kernel
   razor. *Resolution:* the CSV enters as a mapping, not an architecture:
   Planner → Vera's routing + the Proposal service; Executor → the
   hosts (Merlin's seat); Critic/Evaluator → Veda's audit + Gaven's
   gate; Memory → Receipt ledger + State service; Feedback Loop →
   receipts feeding the force law. **Orchestration Layer is the one
   row explicitly not adopted** — routing here is Vera's narrow waist,
   deliberately, and the SOURCES card in the build will say so.
6. **Scale: 30 vs 10.** Raijin's full mandala carries ~30 language
   nodes; the Ball carries 10 worlds, each with real mechanics (phase,
   omega, lattice edge). *Resolution:* 10 stands. The rest are Raijin-
   side surface, not kernel worlds. More worlds is Nathan's call (D-P3),
   not a gap to fill quietly.

## What the gaze mod adds to the examination

Raijin's surface is looked at; the Ball is looked *into*. The gaze rule
Nathan set tonight — hover anything with a label, get a small live
readout of what's happening inside it — has no counterpart in the Raijin
build, and it is the prototype's one genuinely new interaction. It
respects the cross-examined law: a readout is a signal about state,
computed from the same kernel the dock reads; it grants nothing, fires
nothing. Details live in the gaze card precisely because the dock can't
hold them (Nathan, 19:12).
